# 🌾 Harvest Valley

**Harvest Valley** is a cozy pixel-art farming and life simulation game focused on exploration, farming, relationships, progression, and building a life at your own pace.

You arrive in a peaceful valley with a small piece of land and very little to your name. From there, the story is yours to shape.

Grow your farm. Meet the people who live nearby. Explore forests, lakes, mines, and forgotten places. Experience changing seasons, dynamic weather, local events, and the small moments that make the valley feel alive.

---

## About

Harvest Valley is being built as a modern take on the classic farming and life-sim genre.

The goal is to create a world that feels relaxing to live in while still giving the player meaningful progression, exploration, customization, and secrets to discover.

The game combines:

- Farming
- Exploration
- Character relationships
- Fishing
- Mining
- Crafting
- Resource gathering
- Farm customization
- Dynamic seasons
- Weather
- Shops and economy
- Quests
- Festivals
- Hidden locations
- World progression

---

## Core Gameplay

### 🌱 Farming

Prepare the soil, plant seeds, water your crops, and harvest them when they are ready.

Different crops can depend on:

- Season
- Weather
- Soil quality
- Water
- Growth time
- Fertilizer

The farming system is intended to expand beyond simply planting and harvesting.

---

### 🏡 Build Your Farm

Start with a small piece of land and gradually transform it into your own farm.

Planned customization includes:

- Crop fields
- Paths
- Fences
- Decorations
- Buildings
- Trees
- Animal areas
- Storage
- Workshops

Every farm should be able to look different depending on how the player wants to play.

---

### 🍂 Seasons

The valley changes throughout the year.

Planned seasons:

- Spring
- Summer
- Autumn
- Winter

Each season can affect:

- Available crops
- Environment
- Weather
- NPC schedules
- Wildlife
- Fishing
- Events
- Shops
- Exploration

---

### 🌦 Weather

Weather will influence both the atmosphere and gameplay.

Examples:

- Sunny
- Cloudy
- Rain
- Storm
- Fog
- Snow

Some events, resources, fish, and locations may only appear during specific weather conditions.

---

### 🧑‍🌾 Residents

Harvest Valley is home to its own community.

Every important NPC is planned to have:

- Personality
- Daily schedule
- Unique dialogue
- Relationships
- Favorite items
- Personal story
- Special events
- Changing behavior

NPCs should feel like residents of the world rather than static characters waiting for the player.

---

### ❤️ Relationships

Build friendships with the people living in the valley.

Relationships may unlock:

- New dialogue
- Personal stories
- Gifts
- Quests
- Locations
- Events
- Gameplay bonuses
- Romance

---

### 🌲 Exploration

The farm is only one part of Harvest Valley.

The surrounding world may include:

- Village
- Forest
- Mountains
- Lakes
- Rivers
- Caves
- Mines
- Beaches
- Abandoned buildings
- Hidden areas

Exploration will reward curiosity.

---

### ⛏ Mining

Travel underground to search for:

- Stone
- Ores
- Gems
- Rare materials
- Artifacts
- Hidden rooms

Deeper areas may contain increasingly valuable resources and greater challenges.

---

### 🎣 Fishing

Different fish can appear depending on:

- Location
- Time
- Season
- Weather

Fishing is intended to have its own progression system instead of serving only as a secondary activity.

---

### 🔨 Crafting

Resources collected throughout the world can be transformed into useful items.

Examples:

- Tools
- Machines
- Furniture
- Decorations
- Farming equipment
- Consumables

---

## World Simulation

The long-term goal is for Harvest Valley to feel like a living place.

Systems are planned around:

- Day and night cycles
- Time
- Seasons
- Weather
- NPC schedules
- Crop growth
- Shops
- Events
- World changes

The world should continue moving even when the player is focused on something else.
